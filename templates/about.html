Why MealBridge exists

A bridge between extra and enough.

Good food is prepared every day that never reaches a plate. MealBridge helps local donors and verified community organizations act while that food is still fresh.

A practical way to care

Restaurants, hotels, hostels, and event teams can post safe surplus in minutes. NGOs can discover nearby food, request a pickup, and follow its status from acceptance through completion.

Every donation includes clear quantities, preparation and expiry times, and pickup details—so partners can coordinate with confidence.

Trust through verification

An administrator reviews NGO accounts before they can request food.

Rescue before expiry

Live countdowns bring time-sensitive donations to the front.

Local coordination

Search by area, compare nearby pickups, and share precise map coordinates.
